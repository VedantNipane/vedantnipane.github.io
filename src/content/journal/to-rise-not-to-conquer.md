---
title: "To Rise, Not to Conquer"
date: 2025-05-27
type: poem
tags: [healing, gratitude, faith]
excerpt: "In the quiet aftermath of hardship, a soul seeks not revenge or recognition, but restoration through humility. This poem traces a journey of inner rebirth, where gratitude replaces pride, love answers pain, and healing becomes a shared act."
---

Grant me strength to rise once more,  
Not to conquer, but to restore.

With a withered heart and weakened will,  
Through winding paths, I climb the hill,  
To seek the ancient oak tree's shade,  
Where countless weary souls have prayed.

"Grant me strength to rise once more,  
Not to conquer," I asked, "but to restore."

"In this journey of rebirth and grace,  
I seek not glory, but a humble place.  
To honor those who've lit my way,  
With gratitude, I humbly pray":

May I breathe again,  
Not to claim the air as mine,  
But to share life's essence with those,  
Who gave me breath in darker times.

May I be alive again,  
Not to kill the demons that anguished me,  
But to smother those with love,  
Who stood by in loyalty.

May I be aware again,  
Not to dwell in self-reflection,  
But to notice those silent souls,  
Who guided me to introspection.

May I be courageous again,  
Not to crush the timid one,  
But to lift those freaks and feet,  
Who falter in life's long run.

May I be victorious again,  
Not to stash the foe's efforts in vain,  
But to reward those souls,  
Who stood behind me, loving, living sane.

May I speak up again,  
Not to shout till eternity,  
But to say thank you to those,  
Who sang me to sleep so gently.

May I be the vase again,  
Not to show how beautiful I am,  
But to shelter those who gathered near,  
When my soul was shattered and disbanded.

May I grow again,  
Not to reach some lofty height,  
But to lift up those around me,  
Who nurtured me through day and night.

May I be wise again,  
Not to boast of knowledge gained,  
But to guide those seeking light,  
Through shadows where I once remained.

And when my time of service ends,  
May I return unto your light,  
Not as one who conquered darkness,  
But as one who shared your sight.
